AI Disruption Recovery & Asset Response Orchestrator
Problem Statement
When a storm closes a runway, a signal fails on a rail corridor, or a critical asset breaks down, operational data is scattered across airline, airport, rail, and maintenance teams. Operators juggle crews, assets, and rebooking by hand, while travelers get inconsistent information and crowd help desks. The delay between detecting a failure, understanding its cascading impact, and coordinating recovery drives up cost and passenger frustration.

Challenge
Build a multi-agent AI platform that detects disruptions, simulates downstream impact, proposes recovery plans, triages the underlying infrastructure failure, and keeps affected travelers informed. The system should explain the trade-offs behind each plan, adapt as new information arrives, and let human controllers approve or override critical decisions.

Expected Capabilities
•
Disruption detection from weather, flight/train status, and asset-health feeds
•
Cascading impact analysis (missed connections, crew duty-time limits, priority passengers)
•
Recovery plan generation for rebooking, rerouting, and alternate modes under cost, inventory, and transit-rule constraints
•
Infrastructure fault triage, automated work-order creation, and repair-window estimation
•
Proactive, personalized traveler notifications and self-service rebooking
•
Dynamic re-planning as conditions change
•
Trade-off explanations and confidence scores for each recommendation
•
Operations controller-in-the-loop approval workflow
Minimum Scope -must work in the demo
1.
Detect a disruption from a simulated feed (weather or asset-health event)
2.
Show the impacted flights/trains, passengers, and crew
3.
Generate at least two recovery options with trade-offs and confidence scores
4.
Controller approval screen, followed by notifications to affected travelers
Bonus -optional
•
Multilingual voice agent for kiosk or phone-based rebooking
•
Vision agent that estimates queue and crowd density from camera feeds to reroute passengers
•
Automated work-order creation with a repair-window estimate
Suggested Data
A mock schedule of 50–100 flights/trains with passenger bookings and crew rosters, a public weather API, and a simulated asset-health event stream.


AI Cashless Claims, Discharge & Claim-Status Copilot
Problem Statement
Cashless hospitalization often stalls on paperwork. Pre-authorization requests bounce back for missing or inconsistent documents, patients wait hours or days for discharge approval, and insurance reviewers are overloaded with claims that are either clearly incomplete or clearly valid. At the same time, fraud, inflated billing, and medically unnecessary stays must still be controlled.

Patients and hospitals also lack visibility into claim progress: no one can easily see which stakeholder holds the claim, what action is pending, or why it is delayed. This leads to repeated follow-ups and delayed settlements.

Challenge
Build an AI-powered claims copilot that ingests structured and unstructured documents (prescriptions, reports, bills, policy documents), validates coverage, assesses medical necessity, flags anomalies, and communicates status to patients and hospitals. The system should explain every decision, cite policy clauses and clinical guidelines, show confidence scores, and route ambiguous cases to a human reviewer before final submission.

Expected Capabilities
•
Document extraction and structuring from messy, multi-format inputs
•
Policy coverage checks (waiting periods, exclusions, sub-limits, network status)
•
Medical-necessity and length-of-stay validation against clinical guidelines
•
Billing audit (inflated charges, duplicate claims, document mismatches)
•
Faster pre-authorization and settlement through automatic completeness checks
•
Explanations with citations to policy clauses and guidelines
•
Claim journey tracker showing the current owner, pending action, and reason for delay
•
Plain-language patient updates on what is missing, covered, and owed
•
Claims reviewer-in-the-loop workflow with a full audit trail
Minimum Scope -must work in the demo
1.
Extract key fields from a claim packet (bills, discharge summary, policy)
2.
Check coverage against policy rules, citing the exact clause
3.
Flag missing documents and billing anomalies
4.
Claim status view (owner, pending action, delay reason) and a reviewer screen to approve, query, or reject
Bonus- optional
•
Length-of-stay validation against a guideline table
•
Vision agent for handwritten prescriptions and scanned bills
•
Multilingual voice agent for claim-status queries
Suggested Data
A public sample health-insurance policy wording (PDF), 10–20 synthetic claim packets, and a short guideline table for common procedures.
